Sola

A medieval/souls-like looking charcter based off of a drawing I made in the back of my book in english class, it is my first properly rigged charcter. The name sola comes from the latin translation of "alone" which kind of fit the fibe of the render.

Dandon

The concept was from another doodle in a book, more simple character design but the render is a culmination of what i have learnt from modeling and using blender grease pencil to create a more cartoony/scibbling/doodling vibe over the top of the base render.

The starman

Came from my habit of drawing those little stars on any peice of paper that I had, decided to make a whole guy out of it.
The actual mesh only consists of the Planets while the rest is grease pencil that I added to the rig and gave a glow and pixelate filter
