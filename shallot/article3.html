Is your spirit unclean, find out!

Have you been experiencing an eerie sense of dread lately? Are you waking up every morning feeling like a 17th-century witch about to be tried for crimes you definitely committed? Do houseplants wither when you enter the room? If so, it may not be seasonal depression—it may be that your spirit is UNCLEAN.


Step One: Basic Symptom Check

Before we jump to conclusions, let’s start with a quick self-assessment. Ask yourself the following questions: Do you hear whispering voices in the walls that grow louder when you eat dairy? Has your reflection started winking at you when you’re not in a winking mood? When you hold a glass of water, does it tremble, not from your hands, but from an unseen force that despises hydration?

Step Two: Testing the Water (Literally)

Find the nearest body of water. It can be a bathtub, a lake, or even a bottle of Fiji if you’re fancy. Now, gently dip your pinky toe into it. Did the water recoil in horror? Did it audibly hiss like a vampire meeting the sunrise? If yes, you are radiating an energy so foul that even H2O—a molecule designed to sustain life—wants nothing to do with you.

Step Three: The Animal Kingdom's Verdict

Animals have an uncanny ability to sense the supernatural. Take a moment to analyze their reactions: Do dogs bark at you, then immediately apologize as if they’ve just insulted a dark lord? Have you noticed crows forming eerie, cult-like formations above your house, spelling out ominous messages like “Cleanse thyself”? Has your childhood goldfish returned from the dead just to glare at you in disappointment? If any of these sound familiar, your spiritual hygiene is in ruins.

Step Four: Unexplained Phenomena

Now, let’s assess some general weirdness in your life: Are your electronic devices glitching specifically when you try to text your therapist? Do your candles flicker aggressively when you think negative thoughts, spelling out rude messages in Morse code? Have you caught yourself speaking in tongues while casually brushing your teeth? Listen, if you’re experiencing full-blown poltergeist activity every time you order takeout, it’s safe to say your spirit is dirtier than a {joke removed since this is a school project}.

Step Five: Attempting to Fix It

If your spiritual grime is reaching hazardous levels, here are a few quick solutions: Take a bath in salt, holy water, and essential oils marketed by suspiciously enthusiastic Instagram influencers. Burn sage, lavender, and a printed PDF of every email you’ve left unanswered. Summon a priest, a shaman, or a suburban mom with a deep knowledge of essential oils—whoever can perform a full-body exorcism via Facebook Live. If none of these work, you may be beyond saving. But hey, embrace it! Start charging people to use your house as a haunted Airbnb. Lean into the chaos. Maybe you were never meant to be clean. Maybe you were destined for something greater... something eldritch... something that lurks in the shadows and speaks in riddles no human mind can comprehend.



Now go forth, unclean one. And remember: the first step to fixing the problem is admitting that you’re a walking spiritual health hazard. Or, you know, just learn to live with the whispering voices.
